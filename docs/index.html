Tips for best results

  • Use good, even lighting and a plain background.
  • Keep one hand fully inside the frame, close enough to fill a good part of it.
  • J and Z involve motion, so a single-frame model can’t recognise them reliably.
  • Works best in a recent Chrome or Edge on a laptop. The first load downloads about 10 MB.
